What it does

Capture

web/capture.js, Scry game bookmark

Loads a finished game by its number with My Table › Load Old Game in your logged-in dominion.games tab, plays it out against a bot, and copies the log and the score screen. Nothing leaves your machine.

Cartographer

cartographer/

Reads every log line with the client's own templates and replays the game: every player's deck, hand, draw and discard piles after each turn, the Supply, the trash and the score. Checked against score screens and DomBot's final scores.

Advisor

advisor/, Profiles

Reads all your saved games: how often you win with a village, draw, +Buy, trashing or an attack in the kingdom, going first or second, by ending, opening and kingdom card, with 95% ranges. What cards do is measured from the logs. Next: opponents' ratings and top players' games for comparison.

Getting started

  1. Install and start the local app: go install github.com/yourconscience/scrying-pool@latest, then scry web.
  2. One time: export the client's log grammar. scry bookmarklet -client prints a bookmark; click it on dominion.games, then run pbpaste | scry client save. These tables belong to 2nd Shuffle, so they stay on your machine.
  3. On Analyze a game, drag the Scry game bookmark to your bookmarks bar, type a game number, and click the bookmark in your dominion.games tab.
Use it on finished games only: the dominion.games Terms of Service ban tools that help in games in progress. Not affiliated with Shuffle iT, Rio Grande Games, DomBot or the Dominion League.
