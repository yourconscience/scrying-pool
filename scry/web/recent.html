Load your latest games

  1. One time: drag this to your bookmarks bar: Recent games
  2. In your dominion.games tab, on the lobby screen, click it. It reads the 25 latest games on your profile, opens this page in a tab of its own and loads the games that aren't saved yet, about 20 seconds each. Leave that tab be while it works.

Update from your profile

  1. One time: drag this to your bookmarks bar: Recent games
  2. opens dominion.games in a new tab. There, on the lobby screen, click the bookmark: it reads the 25 latest games on your profile and sends them back to this page.

This browser keeps the update. Games without a saved log show what the profile says: the result, the scores and the levels. Turns, $ and the first Province come from the logs the local app saves.

Publish for every device

Writes these games to site-data/recent.json in the repository; the site rebuilds in a few minutes. It needs a GitHub fine-grained token for this repository with Contents read and write. This browser keeps the token until you forget it.
