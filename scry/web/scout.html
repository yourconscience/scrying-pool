Scout an opponent

Their latest games against people and their dominion.games profile, set beside yours.

Load their games

Runs in your logged-in dominion.games tab in Brave, between your games: it won't start while you're at a table. It asks the server for their latest two-player games against people (100 at most) and loads them newest first, about 20 seconds each, until this many are saved. A game the server can't replay is replaced by an older one. It stops after an hour.
